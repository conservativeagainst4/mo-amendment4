What is Amendment 4?

Amendment 4 would require any citizen-led constitutional amendment to win a majority in all 8 congressional districts — meaning just one district can veto a statewide majority vote. Amendments proposed by the legislature would still need only a simple majority. Critics argue this creates a two-tier system that guts direct democracy while protecting legislative power.
